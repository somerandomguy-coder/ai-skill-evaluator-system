The role
We're several phases into a multi-year initiative to modernize our frontend — new tooling, a new web repo, and an architecture designed to be dramatically faster to build and iterate on than what came before. The strategy is set and the roadmap exists, with early wins already proving out the approach.

Alongside the technical work, this role has a real human element: helping other product teams across the engineering org actually adopt the new stack. You'll work with engineers on other teams to understand what's blocking their migration and help make the case — practically, not just technically — for why it's worth the move.

What you'll be doing

Contribute to and help execute our frontend architecture strategy, working within an established roadmap that continues to evolve as tooling and industry practice shifts

Support migration efforts across the wider engineering org — partnering with engineers on other teams to spot blockers and help them move onto the new stack

Participate in RFCs and design reviews as a contributor, helping shape and communicate architectural decisions

Mentor engineers at the mid-level and below, sharing context on the new architecture and helping others build confidence in it

Bring a practical point of view on where AI tooling helps frontend development day-to-day

Take on increasing ownership over specific areas of the platform as you build context and trust with the team

About you

Strong, current experience with React, with well-formed opinions on performance and code quality

A generalist frontend background — comfortable across the stack rather than narrowly specialized in one area

Clear, confident communication — you'll be contributing to design reviews and explaining technical decisions to engineers outside your immediate team

Comfort working within an evolving roadmap, where the plan will keep shifting as the broader ecosystem does

Solid exposure to platform, tooling, or shared-infrastructure work — this isn't a pure feature-delivery role, so experience in this space is required, alongside strong frontend fundamentals

About the job
Responsibilities
We are looking for passionate and talented engineers to join us to build up and optimize a real-time, high-performance, large-scale distributed infrastructure for live streaming at TikTok. We will be deeply involved in the developmental lifecycle of critical product features, and collaborate closely with product managers to deliver the best live streaming experience for live streamers and viewers.

Responsibilities:
- Responsible for the construction of TikTok LIVE ecosystem, with a primary focus on supporting user-side products across diverse web/mobile/desktop platforms, and coordinating full-stack systems.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including client and back-end developers, as well as UX/UI designers, to translate design wireframes and concepts into responsive, visually engaging user interfaces.
- Stay at the forefront of industry trends in front-end technologies, proposing and implementing innovative solutions to enhance our products, optimizing applications for ultimate user experience
- Conduct thorough troubleshooting and debugging of issues, collaborating with the QA team to ensure the highest quality and robustness of code.
- Contribute to decision-making processes and development of engineering infra tools or platforms aimed at improving overall efficiency.

Qualifications
Minimum Qualifications:
- Bachelor's or higher degree in Computer Science or related technical fields
- Solid computer foundation and high proficiency in front-end technology, with understanding of at least one framework (React, Angular or Vue.js)
- Proven experience contributing to large-scale projects
- Familiarity with responsive design principles, cross-browser compatibility, perf optimization, frontend engineering tool-chain and industry best practices
- Strong collaboration and problem-solving skills, attention to detail, and a proactive 'can do' mindset
- Experience developing with or using AI coding tools such as Claude Code, Codex, or AI-powered IDE plugins, with practical experience applying AI coding capabilities in real-world software development workflows.
    
Preferred Qualification(s):
- Experience preferably in cross-platform (React Native, Flutter, PC), full-stack (Node.js based), interactive rendering (Three.js, WebGL) or Low-code/No-code system development
